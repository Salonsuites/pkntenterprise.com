Tenean

Tenean is the business operating platform PKNT ENTERPRISES LLC builds for salon-suite owners and service businesses: the phone line, the calendar, customers, money and marketing in one app, with an assistant that works from the business's own facts. We run it at our own studio in Sharon, MA.

Tenean line, call or text: (877) 836-3595 tenean.com

What Tenean includes

  • A business phone line with calls, texts, email and messages in one thread per person
  • A booking link on the business's real calendar, with confirmations and reminders
  • One record per customer: every call, message, booking and note
  • Email and text campaigns, reviews and the business's own website
  • For salon suites: suites and the floor plan, pros and leases, rent and tours
  • For service businesses: estimates, jobs and invoices

Web, iPhone and Android. Set up with you, month to month.
